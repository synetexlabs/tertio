Tertio

Privacy Policy

Overview

Your digital boundaries are sacred to us. Tertio is designed as a warm, micro-ritualistic sanctuary for a busy world. With a philosophy of less user interaction and only three essential tasks a day, Tertio helps you keep your inner peace, boost your focus, regain your energy, and stay organized through small, intentional, and quiet steps—all without compromising your personal privacy. We do not engage in data monetization or cross-site tracking.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is secured, and what control you maintain over your data.

Information We Collect

To deliver a personalized, high-fidelity experience, Tertio processes the following categories of data:

  • Authentication Data: If you secure your account using Google Sign-In, we record your email address and primary profile details (provided by Supabase Auth) to establish your secure identity. Anonymous guest users are authenticated using isolated local device identifiers.
  • Wellness Profile Indicators: Personal parameters configured during onboarding—such as your energy rhythm preference (chronotype), waking/sleeping hours, and wellness intentions—are stored securely on Supabase.
  • Daily Check-Ins & Reflections: Daily completion metrics, notes, and journaling texts written during your exercises are persisted securely to analyze your progress and allow structural planning.

Data Security & Encryption

We implement a robust, enterprise-grade storage architecture to safeguard your personal details:

  • Transmission Safety: All data transferred between the mobile app and our database is strictly encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS (HTTPS) protocols.
  • Database Encryption: All persistent data tables are hosted inside secure PostgreSQL instances operated by Supabase, locked behind encrypted credential access.
  • Entitlement Checks: Feature authorization (such as verifying Pro subscriptions) is conducted securely with dynamic tokens, meaning no credit card numbers or financial details ever touch our systems.

Your Rights & Data Control

We believe in absolute data ownership. In the settings section of Tertio, you can trigger the following actions directly at any time:

  • Purge Local Cache: Instantly wipe all locally cached completion metrics, database rows, and writing drafts from your physical device memory.
  • Sign Out: Instantly end your session, clearing temporary tokens.
  • Delete Account: Permanently and irrevocably delete your profile, email link, and all history from both the cloud database and your device.
